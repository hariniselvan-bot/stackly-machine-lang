We build the machinery behind intelligent companies.

Stackly started with a frustration: brilliant models die in notebooks. We built the platform that carries them into production — and keeps them honest once they get there.

Our Story

From three laptops to a global GPU mesh.

In 2019, our founders were training demand models for a logistics giant on borrowed hardware. The models worked — but shipping them took longer than building them. That gap became Stackly.

Today, over a thousand teams train, deploy and monitor production models on our platform, serving billions of predictions every month across finance, healthcare, retail and industry.

We remain stubbornly focused on one idea: machine learning should feel like engineering, not alchemy.

The Stackly Founding TeamEst. 2019 — Berlin · Toronto · Singapore
MISSION

Make production machine learning boringly reliable.

Every team should be able to train, deploy and trust models without maintaining a research lab and an infrastructure department. We absorb that complexity so our customers can ship.

VISION

A world where every decision is model-informed.

We believe the next decade of software is predictive. Stackly exists to be the default stack that prediction runs on — safe, observable and radically simple to operate.
